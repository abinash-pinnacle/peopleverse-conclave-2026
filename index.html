CONCLAVE VISION & CONTEXT

Transitioning to HR 5.0

The flagship HR Conclave 2026 organized by NMIET B-School serves as a national thought-leadership platform bringing together corporate CHROs, HR practitioners, industry experts, academic scholars, and management delegates.

In an era marked by rapid AI integration, hybrid work ecosystems, and shifting employee expectations, the traditional mandate of Human Resource Management is undergoing a profound structural evolution.

Transitioning beyond conventional administrative functions and Industry 4.0 automation, the conclave explores HR 5.0—where cutting-edge technology and human capability converge with purpose, empathy, cognitive well-being, and organizational performance. The event connects classroom concepts with real workplace realities through keynote addresses, panel discussions, interactive Q&As, and experiential decision-making challenges.

For Industry & HR Delegates

Engage in cross-industry dialogue on talent acquisition, retention, AI integration, and modern compliance. Benchmark workplace practices against leading IT, BFSI, manufacturing, and retail frameworks.

For Students & Academia

Gain direct exposure to leadership viewpoints, participate in competitive presentations, interact during dedicated Q&A sessions, and build high-value professional networks for future careers.

STRATEGIC GOALS

Key Objectives & Strategic Goals

Curated to provide actionable leadership models, academic alignment, and experiential behavioral development.

Objective 01

Deciphering HR 5.0

Understand the interplay between cognitive technology, human potential, and ethical workforce management. Exploring where automation meets empathy to empower organizational resilience.

  • Cognitive AI & workforce intelligence
  • Ethical governance and algorithmic transparency
  • Human-centric performance architectures
Objective 02

Industry-Academia Collaboration

Align academic curricula directly with current corporate expectations, leadership paradigms, and technological shifts, ensuring zero-day productivity for management graduates.

  • Corporate skill graph integration
  • Live simulations and industry mentorship
  • Pre-placement readiness and internship pipelines
Objective 03

Workplace Dignity & POSH

Promote safe, inclusive, and psychologically healthy environments going far beyond mandatory legal compliance to establish proactive cultures of dignity, respect, and trust.

  • Proactive POSH cultural frameworks
  • Internal Committee (IC) best practices
  • Hybrid & remote workplace safety protocols
Objective 04

Behavioral Decision Making

Enhance critical thinking and strategic judgment through experiential activities like "Risk It, But Think Smart", simulating real-world organizational crises and trade-offs.

  • Crisis response and workforce impact mapping
  • Real-time feedback from CHRO panellists
  • High-pressure decision simulations
Objective 05

Ethical Leadership & Well-being

Balance organizational growth goals with employee wellness, fairness, equity, and inclusion, creating sustainable, high-trust workplace cultures in high-velocity environments.

  • Holistic mental health and anti-burnout policies
  • Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI) metrics
  • Sustainable leadership and psychological safety

Featured Thematic Panel Sessions

Deep-dive discussions led by prominent CHROs, industry legal experts, and people transformation leaders.

PANEL 01 • 12:00 PM

"People + Technology & AI: Redefining the Future of HR!"

Focuses on leveraging AI models, predictive analytics, and automated talent tools while safeguarding human empathy, trust, and corporate culture.

Moderator Ms. Jayashree Mishra — Head HR, Deloitte
Panel Members
  • Mr. Subashish Das — Director, ADRENT Steel
  • Ms. Rosy Das — AABSyS IT
Key Discussion Pillars:
  • AI-driven Recruitment: Speed, precision, and minimizing candidate drop-off
  • Ethical HR Algorithms: Eliminating bias in performance and promotion models
  • Augmenting HR Leadership: Using generative AI co-pilots without losing human judgment
  • Digital Employee Experience (dEX): Frictionless hybrid workflows
  • Balancing Tech with Mental Health: Preventing hyper-connectivity fatigue
PANEL 02 • 03:00 PM

POSH & Modern Workplace: "Beyond Compliance: Building Inclusive Workplaces"

Examines how Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) guidelines can evolve into a proactive cultural framework for workplace respect and psychological safety.

Moderator Dr. Adarsh Rath — Entrepreneur
Panel Members
  • Dr. Isha Mishra — Associate Professor, KIIT
  • Mr. R.K. Mohapatra — Former HR Head, IOCL
  • Mr. Parthasarathi Mishra — Ex-CHRM, Tata Steel
Key Discussion Pillars:
  • Internal Committee (IC) Leadership: Fair, impartial, and trauma-informed inquiries
  • DEI Integration: Embedding dignity across gender identities and neurodiversity
  • Hybrid Workplace Harassment Dynamics: Regulating digital comms and virtual conduct
  • Building Speak-Up Organizational Cultures: Zero retaliation policies and active bystander training

HOST INSTITUTION

About NMIET B-School & Department of Management

Dedicated to developing competent, ethical, and future-ready management professionals equipped to meet the evolving demands of the corporate world.

The Department of Management at NMIET, Bhubaneswar, with a strong emphasis on academic excellence, practical learning, and holistic development, strives to create a dynamic learning environment that nurtures managerial competence, entrepreneurial thinking, leadership abilities, and professional integrity.

The B-School focuses on bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and industry practices through interactive classroom sessions, case-based discussions, presentations, group activities, management simulations, industry interactions, and experiential learning opportunities. These initiatives encourage students to develop critical thinking, problem-solving abilities, effective communication, teamwork, and strategic decision-making skills essential for success in contemporary organizations.

NMIET B-School also emphasizes the importance of innovation, entrepreneurship, human resource development, marketing, finance, and emerging business practices. Through management events, student-led initiatives, business competitions, and professional development activities, students are encouraged to explore their potential, strengthen their confidence, and develop a broader understanding of the business environment.

The department aspires to cultivate socially responsible leaders who understand the significance of ethical governance, sustainability, diversity, employee well-being, and responsible business practices. By fostering collaboration between academia and industry, NMIET B-School seeks to prepare students for diverse managerial roles and entrepreneurial opportunities.
